SD卡使用说明：

将"SOUNDS\", "SYSTEM\", "PICS\"三个文件夹复制到SD卡**根目录**。无需复制本说明或其他文件。

SD 卡文件结构：

SD:\
├── SOUNDS\
│   ├── planting.mp3
│   ├── planted.mp3
│   ├── defuse_start_1.mp3
│   ├── defuse_start_2.mp3
│   ├── defused.mp3
│   ├── boom.mp3
│   ├── startup\
│   │   └── startup.mp3
│   └── Easter_eggs\
│       ├── defused\        (拆除成功彩蛋 *.mp3)
│       └── detonated\      (引爆彩蛋 *.mp3)
├── SYSTEM\
│   └── FONT\               (GBK12/16/24.FON + UNIGBK.BIN)
├── PICS\
│   ├── spike_pics\         (状态指示图 *.bin)
│   ├── startup\            (开机动画帧 all.bin)
│   └── *.bmp               (图片浏览用 BMP)



*The materials used in this project are all sourced from the internet.

SD card usage instructions:

Copy the three folders "SOUNDS\", "SYSTEM\", and "PICS\" to the **root directory** of the SD card. There is no need to copy this instruction file or other files.

SD card file structure:

SD:\
├── SOUNDS\
│   ├── planting.mp3
│   ├── planted.mp3
│   ├── defuse_start_1.mp3
│   ├── defuse_start_2.mp3

│   ├── boom.mp3
│   ├── startup\
│   │   └── startup.mp3
│   └── Easter_eggs\
